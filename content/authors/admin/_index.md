I am a Computer Science Lecturer at University of Reading (UoR). My research interest lies Computer Vision & GenAI methods. I also have a strong practical sense and enjoy working in multidisciplinary projects. At UoR, I am involved in "Heritage & Creativity" projects using GenAI for virtual reconstruction of heritage objects and for generation of synthetic avatars; I also involved in "Climate and Nature" projects for visual understanding of climate data and graph-based ML for biodiversity data. I am active member of ACM SIG-MM communities (MMSys, IMX, QoMex, WebMedia). Previously, I was a postdoc at UCL (UK) working on immersive media, and PhD at PUC-Rio (Brazil) contributing to Media Industry standards (ITU-T SG16, SBTVD).
